SPI / I2C / UART (Internal ECU Communication)
--------------------------------------------------------
These are electronics-level protocols, often used inside ECUs.


Where used?
✔ Sensors
✔ EEPROM
✔ Accelerometers
✔ IMU
✔ Memory chips
