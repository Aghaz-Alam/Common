1️⃣ Active Safety
🔹 Purpose
Avoid or mitigate accidents when risk is imminent.

🔹 Typical Functions
AEB (Automatic Emergency Braking)
FCW (Forward Collision Warning)
LKA (Lane Keeping Assist)
ESC / Traction coordination

🔹 Characteristics
Reactive
Fast, deterministic
Often ASIL-D / ASIL-C
Can override planner decisions

🔹 Example
Obstacle detected at short TTC → emergency braking triggered





2️⃣ AV Planner
🔹 Purpose
Decide vehicle motion based on environment and goals.

🔹 Responsibilities
Path planning
Speed planning
Maneuver selection
Constraint satisfaction

🔹 Characteristics
Predictive
Uses perception + maps + rules
Medium determinism
Never directly actuates

🔹 Example
Plan lane change with safe gap and legal speed







3️⃣ Controls
🔹 Purpose
Convert decisions into physical vehicle actions.

🔹 Responsibilities
Longitudinal control (brake, throttle)
Lateral control (steering)
Actuator coordination

🔹 Characteristics
Hard real-time
Strongly safety-critical
ASIL-C / ASIL-D
Executes commands from:
Planner
Active Safety
SSM (highest priority)

🔹 Example
Apply 30% brake torque within 50 ms




Longitudinal control = controlling the vehicle forward/backward speed, i.e., acceleration and deceleration.
Brake → slows down the vehicle.
Throttle → speeds up the vehicle.

Throttle specifics:
In internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicles, the throttle is a valve that controls the air intake into the engine, which determines engine power.
In electric vehicles (EVs), throttle usually controls motor torque output to accelerate the car.
In simpler terms: more throttle → more acceleration; less throttle → less acceleration.

Example:
Cruise control or adaptive cruise control uses throttle to maintain speed when the car needs to speed up and brake to slow down.

4️⃣ SSM – Safety State Manager
🔹 Purpose
Protect the vehicle when something goes wrong.

🔹 Responsibilities
Monitor health of:
Planner
Active Safety
Controls
Sensors & comms

Detect:
Faults
Inconsistencies
Timing violations

Trigger:
Fallback
Minimal Risk Maneuver (MRM)
Safe stop

🔹 Characteristics
Supervisory
Always active
Highest safety authority
Maps directly to vehicle safe states
